### 🏛️ About Me

I am a **Digital Transformation Strategist**, **Government Officer** (NABARD, Digital Strategy & Innovation), and **Civic Technologist** dedicated to re-imagining how large-scale public institutions deliver digital value. 

Prior to working on national-scale digital infrastructure, I honed my engineering and advisory craft at **Microsoft** and **PwC**. When not architecting public systems and digital platforms, I build developer tooling, curate civic open data, and obsess over command-line workflows.

- 🌐 **Public Sector Innovation**: Driving digital public infrastructure, API-first architecture, and agile transformation across public banking and rural finance.
- ⚡ **Developer Experience**: Passionate about terminal productivity, developer ergonomics, shell automation, and Unix workflows.
- 🇮🇳 **Civic Tech & Open Data**: Believer in open governance, interoperable public APIs, and community-led open source initiatives.
- 💡 **Mentorship & Ecosystem**: Advocating for modern web standards, security-first architectures, and tech literacy.
